      About the 25-year figures

      These figures are our best estimate, based on what you’ve told us and today’s electricity rates.

      They compare the price of the system with the electricity costs it is expected to save over 25 years, and assume that:

      • your electricity use stays about the same after installation;
      • electricity rates rise 5% a year; and
      • your panels produce slightly less each year as they age.

      Your actual results may be higher or lower. They depend on changes in electricity rates, weather, how your household uses power after switching to solar, system performance, maintenance, and government policies. Many households use more power during the day once solar is installed, which can change their savings.

      We show these figures because many homeowners like to see the long-term numbers. They’re estimates to help you compare your options, not financial advice. Your proposal after the site assessment shows the full breakdown for your home.
